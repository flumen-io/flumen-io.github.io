CoinNoteCollection

Coins & Notes Collection of All Nations

A desktop app for recording and managing your coin & banknote collection from around the world, organized by country and era. Browse your collection on a world map, and record details like face value and issue year.

Get it from Microsoft Store Windows 10 / 11

Features

Organize by Country & Era

Record your collection organized by historical periods, such as dynasties and republics.

Visualize on a World Map

See collected countries and variety counts color-coded on a map — get an instant sense of your collection's reach.

Detailed Records

Track face value, issue year, condition, material, design, and more for every coin.

Obverse / reverse photos

Add photos with built-in cropping. Choose which folder photos are saved to.

Japanese / English

Switch the display language anytime.

Easy Backups

Export and import your data — safe for updates or switching computers.

Filter & Sort

Filter by country, region or type, sort in any order, and choose which columns to show.

Gold / Silver / Commemorative

Tag items as coins or banknotes, and mark gold, silver and commemorative pieces.

Fully Local

Your data stays on your device. Nothing is ever sent anywhere.
